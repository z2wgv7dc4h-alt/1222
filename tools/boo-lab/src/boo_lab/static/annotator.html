How to mark a song

Clock must show the song's full length before you draw — not 0:00 / -.

  1. A role button creates the box; drag fits it. Drag never creates a box.
  2. Same idea later = same figure_id (e.g. riff-A).
  3. Double-click a box = zoom + its A–B loop. Esc / click-away / Space stops the loop.
  4. Tick heard after you looped it — the row checkbox or the right-click menu.
  5. Save drops every unheard box.

VAL songs MAY be pinned by ear; they do not train and do not vote prefer=.

Ignore Guess / Load drafts until you know the song by ear.

Lanes and right-click

  • One lane per role, top to bottom Intro → Outro. A box sits only in its role's lane, so a Riff + Breakdown + Outro stack shows as three bars at once.
  • Click a bar to select its row (or a row to light its bar). Right-click a bar to edit role / figure / heard / unique / inst, Play box, Split at playhead, or Delete.
