# Law

This is a labelling lab. It is not God Tier Metal.

- Four pins: riff, hook, breakdown, solo. Not a full pop form.
- Human pins beat allin1.
- Extract only `match=yes` rows (that GP is that song).
- Do not train a song model on FLACs.
- Do not put FLACs, WAVs, or Guitar Pro files in git.
- Do not scrape tabs.
- When 1222 `riff_bank.extract_fragments_from_file` is importable, use it. Do not grow a second parser.

Default command is `START.bat` → scan → studio.
